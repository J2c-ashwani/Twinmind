Privacy Policy

Last Updated: August 4, 2026

1. Introduction

TwinGenie ("we," "our," or "us") is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, disclose, and safeguard your information when you use our mobile application and services.

2. Information We Collect

  • Account Information: Name, email address, and profile details provided during account setup.
  • Chat & Conversation Data: Messages and interactions with your AI Twin, stored securely to provide personal memory continuity.
  • Voice Data: Audio recordings processed temporarily for voice chat and speech-to-text functionality.
  • App Activity & Growth Insights: Mood check-in logs, streak achievements, and daily challenge interactions.
  • Technical & Device Identifiers: Device type, operating system version, and Firebase Cloud Messaging (FCM) tokens for push notifications.

3. How We Use Your Information

We use the collected information strictly for:

  • Providing personalized AI Twin conversations and long-term memory continuity.
  • Generating weekly emotion trends and daily reflection insights.
  • Delivering proactive check-ins and push notifications.
  • Ensuring platform security, authentication, and fraud prevention.

We do not sell your personal information to third parties or advertisers.

4. Data Storage & Security

  • Data Encryption in Transit: All communications between your device and our servers are encrypted using industry-standard TLS / HTTPS protocol.
  • Data Security at Rest: Database records are encrypted at rest using AES-256 standard encryption.
  • Authentication: Secure authentication handled via Supabase Auth services.

5. Your Data Control & Choices

You maintain full control over your personal data:

  • Clear Chat History: Delete your chat history and memory vectors anytime directly in app settings.
  • Delete Account: Permanently erase your account and all associated data within 30 days.

To submit a manual data request, contact us at hello@fsidigital.ca.

6. Third-Party Service Providers

We rely on trusted cloud infrastructure providers:

  • Supabase: Database and authentication services.
  • AI Language Models (Google Gemini, OpenAI, Groq): AI response processing.
  • Firebase (FCM): Push notification delivery.
  • Google Play Billing: In-app subscription processing.

7. Children's Privacy

TwinGenie is not directed to children under 13 years of age. We do not knowingly collect personal data from users under 13.
